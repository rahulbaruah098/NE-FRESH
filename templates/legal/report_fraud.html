Security Help

01

Report Suspicious Activity

If you notice a suspicious payment request, fake delivery call, wrong order status, unknown login, fake NELOCALS message, or any activity that does not feel right, report it to us immediately.

Our team will review the details and help you protect your account, order, and payment information.

02

What to Include

Clear information helps us verify and resolve your report faster.

  • Order ID, registered phone number, or account email if available.
  • Date and approximate time of the suspicious activity.
  • Screenshots, payment proof, message screenshots, or caller details.
  • A short description of what happened and what action you already took.
03

How to Report

Send your details to our support team. We will acknowledge the report and check the activity against your order, account, payment, or delivery records.

Report by Email

Email us at info@ayanant.com with the details mentioned above.

04

Safety Tips

  • Never share OTPs, passwords, payment PINs, or UPI PINs with anyone.
  • Use only payment options shown on the NELOCALS checkout or delivery flow.
  • Do not trust unknown links claiming refunds, prizes, or urgent account verification.
  • Check order details and delivery partner details before handing over payment.
  • For COD, pay only the final amount shown for your order and ask for confirmation.

Need urgent help with an order?

Open Help & Support or contact us with your order details so our team can assist you quickly.
