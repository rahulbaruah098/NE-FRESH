Frequently Asked Questions

How do I track my NELOCALS order?

Open My Orders, select the order, and check the live status timeline. You can see whether the order is placed, accepted, packed, out for delivery, or delivered.

Can I order fresh products from nearby stores?

Yes. NELOCALS helps you discover available products from nearby stores based on your selected delivery location and service availability.

How are delivery fees calculated?

Delivery fees may depend on store distance, order value, operational cost, and delivery availability. The final fee is shown clearly before placing the order.

Can I pay cash on delivery?

Yes, COD may be available for eligible orders. You can choose the available payment option during checkout before confirming the order.

What should I do if an item is missing or damaged?

Contact support with your order ID and product details as soon as possible. Our team will verify the issue and guide you on replacement, refund, or store coordination.

How do I update my delivery address?

Go to your profile or address section, add a new address, and set it as your default address before checkout. This helps stores and delivery partners serve you faster.

How do I report fake calls or payment fraud?

Use the Report Fraud page or contact support immediately. Never share OTPs, passwords, or payment PINs with anyone claiming to be from NELOCALS.
