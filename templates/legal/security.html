01

Security Practices

NELOCALS keeps customer accounts, store activity, cart actions, orders, and delivery data protected through practical access control and secure platform practices.

Account Protection

We recommend keeping your phone number, OTPs, and login details private. NELOCALS support will never ask you to share your OTP or password.

Our safety practices include:

  • Secure customer sessions and controlled access to account areas
  • Order and delivery checks to reduce misuse and wrong handovers
  • Regular review of suspicious activity, abuse, and fraud reports
  • Use of HTTPS and secure handling of form submissions wherever applicable

Always use official NELOCALS pages and avoid sharing account information through unknown links, unknown phone calls, or unofficial messages.

02

Payments & COD Safety

NELOCALS supports a trusted grocery ordering workflow where customers can see delivery charges, order totals, and payment choices before placing an order.

Safe Checkout

Cash on Delivery is available where enabled. Online payment, if available, should only be completed through the official checkout flow.

Payment safety tips:

  • Do not pay through links sent by unknown numbers or unofficial accounts
  • Check the final amount on checkout before confirming an order
  • For COD, pay only after confirming the order and delivery details
  • Contact support immediately if payment is requested outside the platform

NELOCALS does not need your banking password, card PIN, UPI PIN, or OTP for customer support.

03

Report Fraud or Security Concerns

If you notice suspicious calls, fake payment requests, wrong order activity, fake delivery claims, account access issues, or any security concern, report it to our team with clear details.

Security Contact

Email info@ayanant.com with screenshots, order ID if available, time of incident, and a short explanation.

Please include:

  • Your registered phone number or email used on NELOCALS
  • Order ID, product/store details, or delivery details if related to an order
  • Screenshots, call/message details, and approximate time of incident
  • A clear description of what happened and what action you need

We will review reports and take reasonable steps to protect customers, stores, delivery partners, and platform operations.

Need help with account or payment safety?

Send your concern to info@ayanant.com with the required details, and our support/security team will review it.
