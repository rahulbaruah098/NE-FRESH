What We Collect

We collect information you provide directly, plus limited technical data needed to run the service reliably.

Minimum Necessary

We aim to collect only what is required to create your account, fulfill orders, and keep the platform secure.

We may collect:

  • Account info (name, email, phone)
  • Order details (items, amounts, delivery address)
  • Device and usage data (cookies, IP, basic analytics)

How We Use It

We use your information to provide and improve the service, complete transactions, and keep things safe.

Service & Safety

We use data to process orders, prevent fraud/abuse, and support customers.

We use data:

  • To process orders and deliver your items
  • To provide customer support and safety features
  • To improve products, performance, and reliability

Sharing

We share limited data with service providers (for example, payments and logistics) under strict confidentiality and only for the stated purposes.

Limited & Purpose-Based

Providers get only what they need to complete the service (e.g., delivery address for logistics).

We do not sell personal information. If required by law, we may disclose information to comply with legal obligations or protect users and the platform.
