01

What We Collect

Only the information needed to run your NELOCALS shopping experience.

We collect information you provide directly and limited technical information that helps us operate the platform, deliver orders, prevent misuse, and provide customer support.

Minimum necessary collection

We aim to collect only what is needed for account access, product browsing, checkout, delivery, payments, and support.

We may collect:

  • Account details such as name, phone number, email, login and profile information.
  • Delivery details such as saved addresses, selected location, order instructions, and delivery preferences.
  • Order details such as products, store, quantity, price, payment mode, invoices, refunds, and order status.
  • Device and usage details such as browser type, IP address, cookies, session data, and basic performance logs.
  • Support information such as messages, complaints, feedback, fraud reports, and communication history.
02

How We Use It

To help you shop faster, receive orders safely, and get support when needed.

NELOCALS uses information to provide the service, improve reliability, manage orders, and protect customers, stores, delivery partners, and the platform.

Service, safety and support

Data helps us process orders, confirm delivery, prevent fraud, resolve complaints, and improve the NELOCALS experience.

We use data to:

  • Create and manage accounts, sessions, profiles, carts, wishlists, and saved addresses.
  • Process checkout, payments, cash-on-delivery orders, refunds, invoices, and delivery status updates.
  • Share necessary order details with stores and delivery partners for fulfillment.
  • Provide customer support, complaint resolution, fraud reporting, and safety checks.
  • Improve product availability, search, recommendations, performance, security, and reliability.
  • Send order updates, important service notices, and optional promotional updates where permitted.
03

Sharing

We do not sell your personal information.

We share only the information needed to complete an order, provide support, operate the platform, comply with law, or protect users and the business.

Purpose-based sharing

Stores receive order details. Delivery partners receive delivery details. Payment and technology providers receive only what is needed to provide their service.

We may share limited information with:

  • Partner stores so they can prepare and confirm your ordered items.
  • Delivery partners so they can deliver your order to the selected address or location.
  • Payment, hosting, analytics, communication, and security providers that help us run NELOCALS.
  • Legal, regulatory, or safety authorities when required by law or to prevent fraud, abuse, or harm.
04

Cookies & Similar Technologies

Small tools that help the site remember sessions, carts, and preferences.

NELOCALS may use cookies, local storage, and similar technologies for login sessions, cart behavior, location preferences, security, analytics, and performance improvement.

Cookies may help us:

  • Keep you signed in and maintain session security.
  • Remember cart items, selected location, and basic preferences.
  • Measure page performance and understand how users navigate the website.
  • Detect suspicious activity and protect accounts from unauthorized access.
05

Your Controls

You can update key account and delivery details from your profile.

You can review and update your account details, delivery addresses, and order preferences through your NELOCALS account. You may also contact us for privacy-related requests.

Your options include:

  • Update your profile and saved addresses from your account area.
  • Request correction of inaccurate account or order-related information.
  • Request deletion or restriction of data, subject to legal, accounting, safety, and operational requirements.
  • Manage browser cookie preferences through your browser settings.
  • Contact support for questions about privacy, security, orders, complaints, or fraud reports.
06

Data Retention

We keep information only for as long as needed.

We retain information for the time needed to provide NELOCALS services, complete transactions, handle disputes, meet tax/accounting/legal requirements, and protect the platform.

Retention with purpose

When data is no longer required, we delete, anonymize, or securely archive it according to operational and legal needs.

  • Order and invoice data may be retained for accounting, dispute, refund, and compliance needs.
  • Support and complaint records may be retained to resolve issues and improve service quality.
  • Security logs may be retained to protect users, stores, delivery partners, and the platform.
07

Contact

Reach us for privacy questions, corrections, or data requests.

For privacy questions related to your NELOCALS account, orders, delivery information, support requests, or data rights, contact us at info@ayanant.com.

  • Please include your registered phone/email and a clear description of your request.
  • For order-specific privacy issues, include the order ID if available.
  • We may verify your identity before processing account or data requests.

Need help with privacy?

Email us at info@ayanant.com and our team will guide you.
