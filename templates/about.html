About NELOCALS

Celebrating Northeast India Through Every Product

NELOCALS is more than an e-commerce platform—it is a gateway to the rich culture, craftsmanship, and agricultural heritage of Northeast India. We bring together authentic food products, GI-tagged treasures, indigenous textiles, ethnic wear, handicrafts, and unique creations from all eight Northeastern states under one trusted marketplace.

Every product on NELOCALS carries a story. A story of heritage, craftsmanship, natural abundance, and the people behind it — built with authenticity, quality, and fair value for both producers and consumers.
Our Marketplace

Authentic products from local communities, made accessible to customers everywhere.

  • Authentic food products and premium indigenous foods from Northeast India.
  • GI-tagged treasures, spices, traditional textiles, ethnic wear, and handcrafted artifacts.
  • Unique creations from all eight Northeastern states under one trusted platform.
  • Fair value, quality assurance, and genuine market access for local producers.
🤝

Connecting Local Producers

Our mission is to connect local producers, artisans, weavers, Farmer Producer Companies (FPCs), and rural entrepreneurs directly with customers across India and beyond.

🌿

Preserving Heritage

By creating market access for genuine Northeast products, we help preserve traditional knowledge, celebrate cultural diversity, and generate sustainable livelihoods for local communities.

Building a Movement

At NELOCALS, we are building a movement that empowers local communities while allowing the world to experience the true essence of Northeast India—one product at a time.
