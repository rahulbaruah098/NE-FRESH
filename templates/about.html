Chhimphei Women Poultry Producer Company Limited (WPPCL) is the first women poultry producer company of Mizoram, established in 2018.

We are committed to transforming the livelihoods of smallholder producers through a farmer-focused poultry value chain.

SUPPORTED BY MzSRLM
